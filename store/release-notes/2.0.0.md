# What's new in 2.0.0

MindKshetra 2.0 brings a calmer, Stitch-aligned companion experience:

- A complete Home for verse, mood, meditation, sādhana, japa, panchang, paths,
  community, and Madhav.
- A redesigned four-tab experience with Madhav always available.
- Personal guidance in English and Hindi, with improved citations and safety.
- Expanded Jyotish: saved charts, Milan, horoscope, Muhurat, and transits.
- Meditation, path, progress, achievement, journal, and personalization updates.
- Improved accessibility, offline recovery, loading states, and reliability.

MindKshetra offers reflective spiritual guidance and is not a substitute for
professional medical or mental-health care.
